New patients

We're happy to welcome you.

Whether it's been six months or fifteen years, you'll be met with warmth, not judgment.

How it works

Three simple steps.

01

Book your appointment

Call, or use the online form and Danielle will confirm a time that suits you. If you're nervous, say so. It helps us plan a calmer visit.

02

Your first visit

About an hour. We talk first, then a gentle exam and a complimentary 3D scan so you can see what we see. Nothing happens without your say-so.

03

Your personalized care

You leave with clear options, clear costs, and a plan at your pace. No pressure to decide on the spot.

FAQ

Questions we hear often.

I haven't been to a dentist in years. Will I be judged?

No. A long gap is one of the most common things we hear, and it's exactly why we exist. We start with where you are today.

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes. A partner, friend, or family member is welcome in the room if it helps you feel settled.

What is the complimentary 3D scan?

A small handheld scanner takes a digital image of your teeth and gums in a few minutes. No impression material, nothing to bite down on, and you see the result on screen straight away.

Do you speak languages other than English?

Our team is multilingual. Let Danielle know your preferred language when you book and we'll do our best to match you with someone who speaks it.

Can I get an appointment today?

Often, yes. We hold time each day for emergencies and same-day requests. Call as early as you can.
